About the Studio

Architecture rooted in place, refined by craft

Summer Ave Studio is a boutique architecture and development firm based in Great Neck, New York. For nearly two decades, we've been shaping the residential landscape of the North Shore — designing homes that honor the character of this historic Gold Coast community while pushing the boundaries of contemporary design.

Every project begins with the land and the water. Great Neck's unique peninsula geography, tree-lined streets, and proximity to Manhattan inform every line we draw. We don't impose style — we discover it through deep collaboration with our clients and a reverence for the neighborhood's architectural heritage.

Site-Driven Design

Each home responds to its lot, orientation, and the natural features that make Great Neck extraordinary.

Full-Service Delivery

From land acquisition through certificate of occupancy, we manage every phase of development.

Enduring Quality

Materials, systems, and craftsmanship selected for beauty that lasts generations.

Our Process

From vision to keys in hand

1

Discovery & Site Analysis

We begin with an in-depth consultation to understand your goals, lifestyle, and budget. Simultaneously, we conduct a thorough site analysis including zoning review, survey, soil conditions, and environmental factors unique to the Great Neck peninsula.

2

Concept Design

We develop two to three initial design concepts exploring different approaches to your program. These include floor plans, massing studies, and preliminary material palettes presented through 3D renderings.

3

Design Development

The selected concept is refined into a comprehensive design package. Every detail is resolved — from window proportions to cabinet hardware — before construction documents begin.

4

Permitting & Approvals

We navigate Great Neck's village boards, building departments, and architectural review committees. Our established relationships with local officials help streamline the approval process.

5

Construction & Oversight

Our development team manages construction with weekly site visits, progress reporting, and rigorous quality control. We work with a curated network of premium builders and tradespeople.

6

Completion & Handover

Final punch list, systems orientation, warranty documentation, and a celebratory walkthrough. We remain available for ongoing support long after you've settled in.

The Location

Why Great Neck

Perched on a peninsula jutting into the Long Island Sound, Great Neck offers a rare combination: the privacy and natural beauty of waterfront suburban living, just 19 miles from Midtown Manhattan. The LIRR delivers you to Penn Station in 35 minutes.

F. Scott Fitzgerald's "East Egg" was inspired by the grandeur of Kings Point and Great Neck Estates. Today, the area continues to attract families seeking top-rated schools, a tight-knit community, and some of the most desirable residential real estate on the Eastern Seaboard.
