About the Studio

Institutional expertise, personal commitment

Summer Ave Studio was founded on a simple conviction: families moving to Great Neck for its exceptional schools deserve homes designed with the same rigor and vision found in Manhattan's finest developments.

Our founder brings a rare combination of institutional-scale development experience and architectural design leadership — having served as VP of Design at Related Companies and practiced at ODA and Handel Architects, with a Master's in Real Estate Development from Columbia University.

We understand that for many of our clients, a home in Great Neck represents more than real estate. It's an investment in your children's education, your family's daily life, and a community you'll grow with for decades.

Our Process

From vision to keys in hand

1

Discovery & Site Analysis

We begin with your family's priorities — school proximity, commute, entertaining, multigenerational living. Simultaneously, we analyze zoning, lot geometry, and Great Neck's village requirements.

2

Concept Design

We develop two to three design concepts exploring different approaches to your program — presented through detailed 3D renderings so you can see exactly what your home will look like before we break ground.

3

Design Development

Every detail is resolved — from window proportions and stone selection to cabinet profiles and hardware. Nothing is left to chance or field decisions.

4

Permitting & Approvals

We navigate Great Neck's village boards and building departments. Our established relationships with local officials help streamline the approval process and avoid costly delays.

5

Construction & Oversight

Drawing on institutional development experience at Related Companies, we manage construction with weekly site visits, detailed progress reporting, and rigorous quality control.

6

Completion & Handover

Final punch list, systems orientation, and warranty documentation. We remain available for ongoing support long after you've moved in.

The Location

Why families choose Great Neck

Perched on a peninsula extending into the Long Island Sound, Great Neck has attracted discerning families for generations. The Great Neck South school district is consistently ranked among the best in New York State, offering rigorous academics and exceptional extracurricular programs.

F. Scott Fitzgerald's “East Egg” was inspired by Great Neck's grandeur. Today, the area continues to draw families seeking top-rated schools, a safe and welcoming community, and some of the most desirable residential real estate on the Eastern Seaboard.
